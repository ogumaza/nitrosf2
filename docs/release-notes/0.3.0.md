nitrosf2 0.3.0 converts the sounds of Super Princess Peach and the songs of Luminous Arc. It converts the songs of more games at the levels and in the states that the games give them, and plays the pieces of The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks and Phantom Hourglass in the mixes that their scenes give. It also fixes the loops, the reverb send, the pitch of DSE's notes and the level of panned parts.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more cartridge images (`.nds`) or sound archives (`.sdat`) onto `nitrosf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `nitrosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Nitrosf2.app`. Drop images onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New

- **Super Princess Peach.** nitrosf2 converts the sounds of Nintendo SPD's driver into the folder `spd`, each named after its block and its index in the block, such as `01_000`. Each of the sound unit's 16 channels becomes a MIDI track. Eight sounds that only start another sound get no file, and the report names each as an alias. Two pieces, `02_007` and `03_002`, also convert with the flag that sends them on to sections that they don't reach otherwise (`02_007_FLAG1`, `03_002_FLAG1`). The driver's echo goes to CC91.
- **Luminous Arc.** nitrosf2 reads the DSE files of version 0x402 and models the oldest build of DSE's driver, which Luminous Arc runs.
- **Start levels of DSE songs.** Many DSE games start a song below full level. The songs of the Explorers games, Luminous Arc, Fuurai no Shiren DS 2, Fuurai no Shiren 4 and 5, and the Inazuma Eleven games now convert at the level at which the game starts them. Shiren 4, Shiren 5 and the Inazuma Eleven games take the levels from the game's code. A song that the game starts at two common levels gets a second file, named for the second level out of 256, such as `bgm0086_LEVEL256`. In Shiren 5, `BGM_THE_RIIBA_1`, `BGM_THE_RIIBA_2` and `BGM_EVE_JIROKIC_3` convert at the level that the scene sets as it starts them.
- **Poké Transfer's minigame.** Pokémon Black, White, Black 2 and White 2 send a second DS a program whose minigame plays `SEQ_BGM_PALPARK_GAME`. `SEQ_BGM_PALPARK_GAME_HURRY` plays it faster and higher, as in a round's last 20 seconds.

## Changed

- **Beat City's song names.** Each song of Universomo's driver is named after its file, `au` and its number. Song 105, the title screen's music, was `au105_title_screen` in 0.1.0 and 0.2.0, and is now `au105`, like the other songs.
- **The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks.** Five pieces now start in the mix of the scene that plays them: `SEQ_18`, the train's music, in the mix of the train at full speed, and `SEQ_6`, `SEQ_65`, `SEQ_80` and `SEQ_153` in their scenes' mixes. Fifteen new pieces contain the other mixes that a scene keeps for a while: `SEQ_18_SLOW`, `SEQ_18_STOPPED` and `SEQ_23_STOPPED` for the train at a lower speed and at rest, `SEQ_80_CLOSER` and `SEQ_80_CLOSEST` for a chase's distance, `SEQ_65_HEIGHT2` to `SEQ_65_HEIGHT4` for higher places in its scene, `SEQ_6_FULL` for the second state of its scene, and the `_PHASE2` pieces of `SEQ_27`, `SEQ_87`, `SEQ_88`, `SEQ_96`, `SEQ_97` and `SEQ_153` for the second phase of their scenes. `SEQ_18` also leaves out the track that the game mutes, and `SEQ_18_INTRO` plays the piece as the game starts it with nothing playing, on the assumption that the train stands at a station: the intro with every track at first, the tracks of the train at rest muted after two seconds, then a fade to the mix of the train at rest. In `SEQ_184`, `SEQ_194`, `SEQ_251` and `SEQ_304`, the notes on the channels that a cutscene's streamed sound takes now stop when the game takes them.
- **The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass (USA).** `SEQ_103`, the fog at sea, plays without the layer that only the Ghost Ship's approach brings in, and `SEQ_103_GHOST_SHIP` plays it with that layer. `SEQ_101_STOPPED` is the sea music of a ship at rest, slower and with half of its instruments.
- **Panned parts.** On the DS, a sound panned fully to one side plays 3 dB louder than at the centre, and a SoundFont zone can't play above its sample's level. Every SoundFont now plays 3 dB under the game, whatever its zones' pans, and the parts of a sequence keep the balance that the game gives them. In 0.2.0, a part panned to one side played up to 3 dB quieter than the game against a part at the centre.

## Fixes

- `SEQ_39_INTRO` and `SEQ_40_INTRO` of The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks now play the intro with every track, then fade to the piece's mix, as the game does. 0.2.0 played the intro in that later mix from the start, without some of its instruments.
- The `_FINAL_LAP` pieces of Mario Kart DS now play a semitone higher, as the game plays a course's music on the final lap. 0.2.0 played them faster only.
- Each channel of a MIDI file now starts with CC91 0 where the game plays no reverb: in the songs of SDAT games that don't start the SDK's capture reverb, and in every song of DSE and of Universomo's driver. A player that follows General MIDI 2, such as BASSMIDI, gave a channel without CC91 its default reverb send of 40, which the game doesn't play.
- Looping songs now loop on a pass whose repeat plays as the game does. A song chooses its loop by what each note plays: its pan, its level, its pan LFO's depth and the bend range of a slide. Where the game's first pass differs from the later ones, as in `SEQ_BGM_SW_R_23` of Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 or `BGM_LAST_BOSS_NEKURO` of Kirby Mass Attack, the song now loops on the second pass and is about twice as long. 0.2.0 looped the first pass, and its repeats played some notes at another pan, level or slide than the game. A few songs whose passes play alike now loop on the first pass and are half as long, such as `SEQ_BGM_DATA_CONV` of Pokémon Black 2 and White 2.
- A note that starts while another note of its track still sweeps its pitch now plays on a MIDI channel of its own, and the held note keeps its pitch. Some battle themes of the four Pokémon games of Unova and some sound effects of Spirit Tracks, The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Anniversary Edition and Kirby Mass Attack change.
- DSE's driver drops the fraction of each channel's timer period, so its high notes play sharp of their keys. A DSE song's SoundFont now tunes each key that the song plays to the driver's pitch, and the SFZ files tune every key. A DSE part that sounds one note at a time sends the pitch bend at which the driver plays it, and a note that the part's bend would play more than 5 cents off moves to a part of its own while one is free.
- DSE's driver squares a voice's volume and rounds it down. A quiet voice therefore falls silent while its envelope still runs. nitrosf2 kept such voices sounding, and parts of many DSE songs and sound effects rang on where the game is quiet or silent, up to 25 dB too loud in `bgm0182` of Explorers of Sky. A part's CC11 is now 0 while its voices are silent, and CC120 stops a released part. Only the MIDI files change. Each song keeps its loop start and its loop markers. One file, `BGM_EVE_MYSTIC` of Fuurai no Shiren 4 and 5, now has an event 5 ms past its loop end.

## Known limitations

- Some sound effects of Super Princess Peach's shared block play the instruments of whichever scene is loaded. nitrosf2 can't convert them alone yet. It lists each one as not converted and goes on with the rest. The game's changes of pitch and tempo with Peach's moods stay out.
- Apart from the Explorers games, most DSE games keep their sound effects in a layout of bank that nitrosf2 doesn't read yet. nitrosf2 converts such a game's songs only, and gives a warning for each such bank.
- nitrosf2 doesn't find DSE songs in packs compressed other than with the SDK's LZ77 or in Level-5's GFSA archives. Such an image gives no warning about them.
- Every SoundFont plays 3 dB under the game. SoundFont players interpolate between a sample's points, and the DS doesn't. Samples with a lot of high frequencies play a little quieter in a SoundFont player. The SFZ files play at the game's level and without interpolation.
- The console's reverb is left to the player. CC91 sets the send where a game starts it.
- Fades, and other changes that the game's code makes during play, stay out unless a profile lists them.
- A game that plays its music through another driver, or only as streamed recordings, converts nothing yet.
- See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
